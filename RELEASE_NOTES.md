# Weighted edge recoloring and 1-factorization across a cut

**Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini · Preprint 1.0**

[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24521352](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24521352)

The paper gives weighted recoloring bounds with prescribed class sizes, an exact formula for the deficit of block-constrained completion, and a 1-factorization criterion using weighted Hall reserves, a hypergraph nibble, and simultaneous spectral repair. An explicit sparse grid factors for every even \(q\ge12\) and fails the rectangle criterion on the same cut.

The manuscript and formalization companion are available in English and Spanish. The complete package includes all four PDFs, their LaTeX sources, the pinned Lean 4.35.0-rc3 development, the internal audit, and the computational evidence. The Lean archive can also be downloaded separately.
